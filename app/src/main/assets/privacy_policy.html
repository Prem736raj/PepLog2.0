Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Scope

PepLog ("we", "our", or "the App") is a personal tracking and organization tool developed by Appvexis. This policy describes the information the App stores and the optional services it uses.

2. Information stored on your device

Protocols, dose logs, inventory, biomarkers, progress photos, calculator presets, preferences, and locally imported health metrics are stored in the App's private Android storage. PepLog has no PepLog-operated account or application server. Android app isolation and device security protect this local storage. The optional App lock uses the device's existing screen lock; PepLog never receives or stores your PIN, password, or biometric template. The lock is an access gate, not a custom database-encryption layer.

3. Health Connect

If you grant Health Connect permissions, the App reads only the health record types shown in the permission screen and processes them locally for the App's charts and logs. Health Connect access is optional and can be revoked in Android Health Connect settings.

4. Diagnostics

Release builds suppress debug logs and may write warning or error messages to the Android system log for troubleshooting. No third-party crash-reporting service is bundled in this build, and PepLog does not intentionally put dose values, notes, photos, or health records in log messages. Debug builds may write technical logs visible to a development tool.

5. Exports and sharing

PepLog can create CSV, JSON, clinician-summary PDF, or photo-inclusive ZIP backups in its private app cache when you request one. The App does not upload an export by itself. Android's system share sheet lets you choose a destination, and that destination's own terms and privacy policy then apply. Exports can contain sensitive health information, notes, timestamps, and (for ZIP backups) private progress-photo copies; review them before sharing.

6. Notifications

If you grant Android notification permission, PepLog may run local background checks for upcoming dose reminders and inventory/expiration alerts. These notifications are generated from records stored on your device; no reminder content is sent to a PepLog server. You can revoke notification permission or disable the notification channels in Android settings.

7. Information we do not use

  • No location tracking or advertising SDK is used.
  • PepLog does not sell personal or health information.
  • PepLog does not run a social profile or public health-data feed.
  • This build makes no PepLog network requests. Health Connect data is read through Android's local Health Connect service.
  • PepLog has no subscriptions, in-app purchases, advertising, or payment-data collection.

8. Retention and deletion

Clear the App's storage or uninstall the App to remove its local database, preferences, and cached exports. Shared exports may remain with the recipient or service where you shared them, so delete those copies separately when no longer needed.

9. Children's privacy

PepLog is not intended for children under 18. Do not use the App to store another person's information without appropriate permission.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the App's data practices change. The effective date above will be updated with material changes.

For privacy questions, contact privacy@appvexis.com. Verify this address and publish this policy at a stable public URL before submitting the App to Google Play.
