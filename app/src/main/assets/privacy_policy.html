Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

PepLog ("we", "our", or "the App") is a peptide tracking and health monitoring application developed by Appvexis. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

2. Data Collection

2.1 Data Stored Locally

All your health data, dose logs, protocols, biomarkers, progress photos, and personal tracking information is stored exclusively on your device using an encrypted local database. We do not have access to this data.

2.2 Google Drive Backup (Optional)

If you choose to enable Google Drive backup, your data is encrypted and uploaded to your personal Google Drive account's hidden app folder. Only you (via your Google account) can access this data. We cannot see, access, or retrieve your backup data.

2.3 Google Account Information

When you sign in with Google for backup purposes, we receive your email address and display name solely to show which account is connected. This information is not transmitted to any server.

2.4 Health Connect Data

If you grant Health Connect permissions, the App reads health metrics (weight, sleep, heart rate, blood pressure, steps, body fat) from Health Connect. This data is processed locally on your device and is never transmitted externally.

2.5 Subscription Information

Subscription management is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not collect or store any payment information. Google Play manages all billing, receipts, and payment data.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect personally identifiable information (PII)
  • We do not track your location
  • We do not sell your data to third parties
  • We do not use advertising SDKs
  • We do not collect analytics on your health data content
  • We have no backend servers that receive your data

4. Data Security

Your data is protected by:

  • On-device encrypted database (SQLCipher + Android Keystore)
  • Encrypted Google Drive backups
  • Google Play Integrity API verification
  • No network transmission of health data

5. Data Retention & Deletion

All data is stored locally on your device. You can delete all app data at any time by:

  • Clearing app data in Android Settings
  • Uninstalling the app
  • Using the in-app data management options

Google Drive backups can be deleted from your Google Drive settings.

6. Children's Privacy

PepLog is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the app and on our listing page.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at: privacy@appvexis.com
